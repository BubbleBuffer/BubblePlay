BubblePlay for Windows: UI mockups

OverviewConnect flowSession chrome

Static review mockups for the native viewer (egui on D3D11, 2026-10-13). Nothing is built yet. Each page has a state switcher (buttons or number keys) and design notes at the bottom.

Connect flow

Saved PCs (two home variants), add a PC with password and 6-digit code, sign-in error, Cloudflare approval wait, connecting, connect failed.

Session chrome

Collapsed tab, toolbar, monitor picker, stats, capture and release toasts, reconnecting, Admin required, secure desktop.

Direction

The stream owns the window. Chrome is near-opaque warm duvet, attached to the top edge like a cut tab, and only bright apricot says "live" or "captured". Out of session, the app is calm and left-aligned with one confident Bricolage moment per screen and a single apricot action.

What should make it feel better than the browser: native speed (no page loads), keycaps that teach the escape chords, states that always say what survived, and one-click reconnect to the last PC.

Shared tokens

Same family tokens as worker/public/bubbleplay.css; fonts are copies of worker/public/fonts. Fitted radii: control 7 11 6 9, field 6 10 6 8, panel 12 18 10 16, edge strip 0 0 10 12. Spacing on a 4 px base.

Icons

Lucide, verbatim from lucide-static@0.460.0 (ISC), bundled as a sprite in icons.js. In egui: rasterise the same SVGs to textures at 16/18/22 px for 1x, 1.5x and 2x DPI.

Out of v1

File transfer and clipboard are not drawn. Their slot is reserved in the strip between Stats and Settings.

Open questions for the owner

  1. Home screen: A or B? A is a calm list with a detail panel (scales to many PCs). B is a bold stage for the last PC (more character, best with one to three PCs).
  2. Strip height. The browser strip is 32 px; the mockup uses 42 px for larger native targets. Keep 32 for parity?
  3. Stats chord. Ctrl+Alt+Shift+S is a proposal; the architecture page only fixes Z and Q.
  4. Saved PC fields. Is one relay always one PC? The mockup assumes yes and shows no online status, since the client cannot know it without contacting each relay.
  5. Approval wait. Is there a request code to match on the phone and a timeout? None is shown until the protocol defines one.
