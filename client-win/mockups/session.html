Layout

  • All chrome lives on the top edge, centred: a 26 px tab at rest, a 42 px strip when open. Edge-attached (square top corners, fitted bottom corners), not a floating pill.
  • Strip order: connection state, capture state with its chord, Display, Mode, Quality, Audio, Stats, Settings, Leave. Seams separate the four groups; Leave is alone at the end.
  • Popovers drop 6 px below the strip; one open at a time, Esc closes and is not sent to the PC.
  • Stats is corner-attached top right so it never covers the centre of the stream.
  • Windowed mode: the 36 px title bar stays and the tab hangs below it. Post-v1 slots (Files, Clipboard) go between Stats and Settings.

Type scale

  • Recovery title: Bricolage 24/1.05. Toast and banner title: Atkinson 14/720.
  • Strip labels: Atkinson 12.5/720, 32 px targets. Body in cards 13.5/450.
  • Fragment Mono 11 for latency, attempts, modes and all stats values; keycaps Fragment Mono 10-11 with a 2 px bottom thread.

Colour over video

  • Chrome fill rgba(38,40,45,.96), a near-opaque duvet, so labels pass contrast on any frame. Stats panel rgba(16,18,20,.88).
  • Apricot #F0A064 only for live signal and the captured state. Attention #F0D29C for blocked input and secure desktop. Paw #DDA39D marks recovery. Focus stays #7AB7FF.
  • Every state also has a word: Captured, Input blocked, Input paused, Reconnecting.

Motion and egui cost

  • Tab to strip: 180 ms height and fade from the top edge. Toast: 220 ms drop-in, holds 4 s, 180 ms out. Popover: 120 ms fade plus 4 px. Reduced motion: no slide, fades only.
  • Reveal: pointer rests 150 ms within 6 px of the top edge, or a release chord. Hide 1.2 s after the pointer leaves.
  • Reconnecting dim: desaturate the last frame in the video shader (one uniform). Cheap.
  • Optional, costs work: background blur behind the strip and cards (extra downsample pass). The design does not need it; near-opaque fills are the default.
  • egui repaints only on change; the stats sparkline forces a repaint every frame while open. Cap it at 10 Hz.
